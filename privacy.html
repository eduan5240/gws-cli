Privacy policy — gws-cli-eduan

This OAuth client is used solely by its owner, on his own computer, to access his own Google account data.

  • Data accessed through Google APIs is used only to carry out commands the owner runs himself.
  • No data is collected, stored on any server, sold, or shared with third parties. OAuth tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's own machine.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact: open an issue at github.com/eduan5240/gws-cli.
